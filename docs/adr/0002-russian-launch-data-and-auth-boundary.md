# Russian Launch Data And Authentication Boundary

The initial product is operated by a Russian citizen for a Russian audience. Store Russian users' personal data and match history in Russia, target Yandex Cloud for production deployment, and support unique-login/password registration and sign-in. Yandex ID and VK ID remain planned primary social sign-in methods; do not depend on foreign email or treat Telegram OAuth as a compliant primary path without a dedicated legal review. This makes the launch constraints explicit before identity, data, and deployment choices become costly to reverse.
