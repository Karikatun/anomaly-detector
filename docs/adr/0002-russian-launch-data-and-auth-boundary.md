# Russian Launch Data And Authentication Boundary

The initial product is operated by a Russian citizen for a Russian audience. Store Russian users' personal data and match history in Russia, target Yandex Cloud for production deployment, and support unique-login/password registration and sign-in. Yandex ID is the only social sign-in method in the MVP; VK ID and Telegram OAuth require a separate product and legal review before they enter scope. This makes the launch constraints explicit before identity, data, and deployment choices become costly to reverse.
